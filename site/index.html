How prices work

  • Market ea. is a weighted average of whichever sources have a price for that product (weights live in config.json).
  • TCGplayer uses TCGplayer's market price (based on completed sales), refreshed daily. If TCGplayer has no market price at all, it falls back to the middle listing price, marked listing.
  • Value over time comes from TCGplayer's weekly market-price history. Before a product has any price history, it counts at what you paid.
  • Cardmarket is Europe's largest TCG marketplace. It uses the 30-day average sell price from Cardmarket's free daily price guide, converted from euros at the European Central Bank's daily rate (hover a price to see the euro amount).
  • PriceCharting fills in once a PRICECHARTING_TOKEN secret is added to the repo.
  • eBay sold comes from the ebay_price column in collection.csv; enter an average of recent sold listings for items you want it on.
  • check match means the product was matched by name with low confidence; add its TCGplayer ID to the CSV to pin it. sources disagree means prices differ by 25%+.
Prices from TCGplayer (via TCGCSV), Cardmarket, PriceCharting and manual eBay comps. Not financial advice.
